Project creation and invites, with the PRD structure

The member opens a project or accepts an invitation, or walks four guided steps (Where, Structure, People, Idea) and a review to create a project in a new or an existing repository.

PRD section
5 (Screens), row 2 · flows 4.2 (Starting a project) · 7 (Permissions)
Controls
Your projects (recently opened) with Open; pending invitations with one-click Accept; New project and Connect existing, the two ways in. Every step: which step of four, Back, Next, Cancel, and Back to review once the review has been seen. Where: new repository with an Owner picker (your organisations and your own account) and, for your own account only, Private or Public, or the repository list with search and Check access; a switch between the two. Structure: PRD structure source (default of 13 sections), add, remove, rename, reorder sections. People: invite by GitHub login; own role and domains. Idea: text with attachments, or a pasted or uploaded transcript. Review: each step's answers with Change; Create project or Connect and start. Save and regenerate for an existing project.

What Grillo is

The right experts, the right questions, a PRD you can trace

  • Only your questions

    Experts answer only the questions for their domain, live or in their own time.

  • Recorded on your laptop

    Answers are recorded with their discussion, on your own laptop.

  • Every line has a source

    Every line of the PRD traces back to who said it, when and where.

How it works

From idea to PRD in five steps

  1. 1

    Create a project

    Create a project and invite the people who know the subject.

  2. 2

    Give the idea

    Give the idea as text or a transcript; Grillo turns it into a map of tickets and questions.

  3. 3

    Tag the questions

    Run the tagging round so each question reaches the right domain.

  4. 4

    Answer together

    Answer together in a live session, or each in your own time.

  5. 5

    Get the PRD

    When the map is done, Grillo writes the PRD; download it as Markdown or HTML.
