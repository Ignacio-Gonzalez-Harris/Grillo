# Elm Street tool share

## Problem

- Tools go missing from the shed: 6 of the 40 listed tools could not be found at the autumn count. [S6, Q4.1]

## Users and roles

- Garden members borrow and lend tools at the shed screen. [S7, Q6.1]
- The shed keeper also takes tools out for repair or for good. [S7, Q6.1]

## Flows

- A member borrows at the shed by holding their garden key card to the reader. [S2, Q2.1]
- A member keeps a tool for at most a week. [S3, Q2.3]
- The shed screen shows who has each tool right now. [no source]

## Permissions

- Only members who hold a key card may lend tools. [S4, Q3.1]
- Only the shed keeper may take a lent tool out of the shed for good. [S5, Q3.2]
