### Destination
A PRD for a tool-sharing app for the made-up Elm Street community garden.

### Notes
The idea is source S1. The idea says "shed" for the tool store and "key card" for the garden's door badge.

### Settled

- #3 Key-card members lend; the lender takes a tool out for good after telling the shed keeper
